# 02: The composer and the reply box

**What to build:** Flow gains the concept's composer, which starts a thread or creates a task in the chosen channel, and the pane gains a reply box. What the person writes appears at once, marked as sending; a failure says so in place, with Try again and Drop. Every way to write is disabled while the board is not live, with the reason, and read-only channels say so. Mentions settle, and one that reached no one is pointed out.
