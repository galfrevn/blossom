# 06: A middle click opens a link in the browser

**What to build:** a middle click on a link in the pane opens it in the default browser, as a plain click does. Today the window's guard refuses the navigation WebKit tries, and nothing happens (Thread 02).

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [x] A middle click and ⌘-click on a link open it through the shell, with the same check of its scheme as a plain click.
- [x] The window still never leaves its page.
- [x] Tests come first, against the fake core.
- [ ] Verified by hand in the release app.

## Comments

Built on 2026-11-23 by the integrator, test-first, on main at 1ec2251, without a subagent.

**What was built.**
- **The pane opens links from `auxclick` as well as `click`.** WebKit dispatches a middle click as `auxclick`, which nothing listened to, so its default action tried to take the window to the link and the navigation guard refused it. The pane now takes an `auxclick` of the middle button on a link the way it takes a click: it prevents the default and asks the shell to open the link, which checks its scheme again (`open_link`, Thread 02). A right click is left to the system.
- **⌘-click needed nothing.** It is a `click` with `metaKey`, which the pane already took. A test now says so.
- **Opening links moved out of `ui/thread/pane.tsx`** into `ui/thread/links.ts`, with its own tests in `ui/thread/tests/links.ts`. The pane's tests of a plain click stay where they were.

**Tests:** 4 in `ui/thread/tests/links.ts`, against the fake core's `openLink`. Without the `auxclick` listener, the middle click's test fails and the other three pass: ⌘-click, a right click, and a middle click beside a link.

**Checked in the release app,** under a home of its own, with a temporary line in the page that is not in this change: WebKit in the app answers `"onauxclick" in window` with `true`, so it dispatches the event this change listens to, and the navigation guard kept the window on its page when the page tried to leave it.

**Not verified by hand:** a real middle click on a link in the pane. Making one means moving the pointer and clicking on a screen the owner was using, so the last box waits for the owner, or for a session on a screen of its own. Middle-click a link in a thread: the link should open in the browser and the app should stay as it is.
