# 01: The pane opens a thread from the replica

**What to build:** the frame gains the concept's detail pane, beside the lens. Selecting a line in Flow opens its thread there: the root and every reply, oldest first, each with its author, the person it acts for and its time, as plain text for now. The pane follows the thread as replies arrive. Esc closes it.

**Blocked by:** Flow 02

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [x] The core answers a thread from the replica through an index on `(thread, created_at)`, and a subscription that follows it.
- [x] The pane draws the thread as the concept does; selection moves with the arrow keys in Flow.
- [x] Tests come first: the thread in the core, the pane against the fake core.
- [x] Verified by hand on the 100,000-message board: the largest thread opens in under 50 ms.

## Comments

Built on 2026-11-21 by a subagent in its own worktree, test-first, and integrated onto Flow 04. The core gained 7 tests, and the UI 24: the pane 13, Flow's lens 8, the frame 1, and a fold's selection 2.

**Verified by hand:**

- On `vb serve` with two tasks and their agents' progress: selecting a person's reply opened its task's whole thread, 11 messages with every progress update, and marked the line. Opening the `#fleet/api` fold and choosing one of its updates opened that task's thread, 3 messages, and marked the update inside the fold.
- On the board with 100,063 messages, whose largest thread holds 154: reading it took 1 to 2 ms, and opening it, from the click until the pane is drawn, took 15 to 25 ms over ten runs.

Decisions made along the way:

- **No subscription of its own.** The pane reads its thread again on every Flow change, as Flow reads its windows again. That covers "a subscription that follows it". A thread subscription waits until reading the largest thread on every batch shows up in the budgets.
- **The thread is read through `messages_by_thread (thread, created_at)`.** A test checks the plan searches by thread with no sort. The index is part of the versioned schema, which goes to version 3, so a replica from before is copied again.
- **The selected line carries `aria-current`, not `aria-selected`,** which ARIA does not allow on a feed's articles. It takes the accent wash and keeps it on hover, as the concept does.
- **Clicks reach Flow through one listener on the list,** not one per row, so rows stay as light as Flow 02 measured.
- **Folds.** A closed fold stands for its latest update: the arrows select it and open that update's thread, and its line takes the accent wash. An open fold shows each update as a line of its own, outside the fold's button, so a click opens that update's thread. The list hands a row the selection only when the row holds it, so the other rows stay memoized.
- **The arrow keys** move the selection only when nothing else has the keyboard's focus, and not with a modifier held. They scroll the line into view and let go of the bottom. With nothing selected, an arrow selects the line at the bottom of the screen. If the next line's block is not loaded yet, the press does nothing. Search 04's J and K can take this over.
- **Esc closes the pane wherever the focus is.** The finder (Search 02) will need to take Esc first while it is open.
- **Scrolling in the pane.** It opens at the root. At the end of a thread, new replies bring it down to them; further up, it keeps the reader's place. Opening another thread starts again at the root.
- **The header reads "Thread in #channel",** with no title in the body; titles belong to tasks (ticket 03).
- **A message the replica no longer holds closes the pane,** as when another board opens.
- **Plain text keeps its spacing** (`pre-wrap`) until markdown arrives in ticket 02.
- **The pane draws the whole thread, with no windows.** Its messages are memoized, so reading it again redraws only what changed.
- **Left out:** the concept's narrow layout, where the pane covers the lens below 900 px. The sidebar does not do its narrow layout either.

Not measured: Flow's frames at 120 Hz with the pane open.
