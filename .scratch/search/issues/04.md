# 04: A shortcut for everything, and a sheet that lists them

**What to build:** 1 to 4 open the lenses, J and K move through any list, Enter opens, Esc closes, ⌘K opens the finder, and ? opens a sheet of every shortcut. No shortcut fires while the person types in a box.

**Blocked by:** 03

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [x] The shortcuts read the commands' list.
- [x] Moving through a list scrolls the selection into view.
- [x] Tests come first, against the fake core.

## Comments

Built on 2026-11-23 by the integrator, test-first, on main at 54d01c7, without a subagent. The UI gained 13 tests and lost 1: the shortcuts 6 (`ui/tests/shortcuts.ts`), the sheet 5 (`ui/frame/tests/sheet.tsx`), the commands 1, Flow and Tasks 1 each for J and K, and the application 1 for the sheet; the sidebar's test of a lens's key moved to the shortcuts. Every new test failed first.

**What was built.**
- **One hook runs the shortcuts from the commands' list** (`ui/shortcuts.ts`). A key runs the command whose shortcut it is, unless it was typed into a box, pressed with ⌘, control or option, or already taken by another part of the app. Shift is allowed, since `?` takes it to type. The sidebar no longer listens to keys of its own: a lens's key is the command that goes to it.
- **A key belongs to the last command that declares it.** While a channel is chosen, "Show the whole board" is declared after the lenses with the key of the lens on screen, so that key widens the lens, as pressing it did before, and the other keys still go to their lens.
- **`?` shows a sheet of every shortcut** (`ui/frame/sheet.tsx`): the commands, read from the same list, then the keys that move through a list and the finder's. It is a modal over the app, as the finder is. It takes the keyboard while it is open, so no key reaches the app behind it, and escape, `?` or a click beside it closes it. "Show the keyboard shortcuts" is a command too, so the finder lists it with its key.
- **J and K move through Flow and Tasks** as the down and up arrows do, through the same path, which scrolls the selected line or row into view. They stay out of the finder, where they are letters to type: the finder still moves with the arrows only.
- **Shortcuts run only while a board is open,** like the finder, so the start screen takes no keys.

Decisions made along the way:
- **1 to 4 follow the lenses that exist.** Flow is 2 and Tasks 3; Fleet 02 adds 4 to `LENSES`, and Attention will add 1. Nothing else is needed for their commands, keys or lines in the sheet.
- **Enter opens what the finder selected.** In Flow and Tasks, selecting a line or a row already opens its thread, so there is nothing left for Enter to open there.
- **The sheet lists the keys that are not commands from the dictionary,** in `dictionary.shortcuts.keys`. They are keys of the lists, the pane and the finder, not actions the finder could run.
- **The application keeps its choice and its commands in one hook,** `useChoiceWithCommands`, so the component stays under Biome's 50 lines.

**Not checked in the app.** This ticket has no box for it, and opening the sheet in the release app means typing into a window on a screen the owner is using. Its look follows the finder's palette: the same scrim, border, radius and shadow, with the sidebar's small capitals for its two headings.
