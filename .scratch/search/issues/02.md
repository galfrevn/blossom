# 02: The finder searches the board

**What to build:** the title bar gains the concept's finder box. ⌘K, or a click on it, opens the finder over the app. Typing searches the replica and lists messages, tasks, channels and agents with the matched words marked. The arrows move, Enter opens the result in the detail pane, Esc closes. It works offline.

**Blocked by:** 01, Flow 01

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [x] The finder follows the concept's palette.
- [x] Results refresh as the person types, without waiting on a slow query.
- [x] Tests come first, against the fake core.
- [x] Verified by hand on the 100,000-message board, online and with `vb serve` stopped.

## Comments

Built on 2026-11-23 by a subagent in its own worktree, test-first, on `main` at cb7e811. The UI gained 46 tests (41 in `ui/finder` and `ui/thread/tests/openings.ts`, one each for `narrowTo`, the two lenses and two in the application) and went from 185 to 231; 7 were moved, not changed.

**What was built.**

- **The finder box** sits at the end of the title bar, as the concept draws it, while a board is open: "Search the board" with its `⌘K`. A click or `⌘K` opens the finder; `⌘K` again closes it.
- **The finder** follows the concept's palette: a scrim over the whole window, a 560 px box 70 px from the top, the query in 14 px, groups under mono headings, the selected row in the accent wash, and the foot with `↑↓ move`, `↵ open` and `esc close`. The concept's scrim and shadow join `ui/theme/tokens.css` in both themes, as its state colours did with Tasks 02.
- **Results** come in four groups: channels, tasks, messages, then agents. The words that matched are marked in ink at a heavier weight, never in the accent, which ADR 0016 keeps for the selected row. A task shows its state in the state's colour and its channel; a message shows who wrote it, where, and the day; a channel reads as its path; an agent as its name and `@handle`.
- **The keyboard:** the arrows move the selection and go round at either end, the selected row is scrolled into view, Enter opens it, Esc closes, and Tab stays in the box. The keys the finder takes stop there, so Esc never also closes the thread pane behind it. Closing gives the keyboard back to whatever had it before.
- **Opening a result:** a message or a task opens its thread in the detail pane of the lens on screen, through `ui/thread/openings.ts`, which both lenses listen to; a channel narrows the lens to it (`narrowTo` in `ui/frame/choice.ts`, which, unlike the sidebar's choice, never widens a channel already chosen).
- **Queries as the person types** (`ui/finder/searches.ts`): one search runs at a time and only the latest query typed meanwhile waits for it, so the answer to what is in the box never queues behind more than one other search. An answer to an older query is shown while the latest runs, and an empty box shows nothing at once and drops the answer still on its way.

Decisions made along the way:

- **Agents are listed but open nowhere yet.** The spec lists them, and leaving them out made `agent` say "No match" while the core found 40. There is no agent pane until Fleet 03, so an agent's row is shown but disabled, the arrows pass over it, and the group comes last. When the pane exists, agents join the rows that open.
- **Groups go from the fewest to the most:** channels, tasks, messages. A channel or a task found is not buried under 50 messages.
- **The box says "Search the board" and the query "Find a message, a task or a channel"** until Search 03 brings commands, since the concept's "Search or run a command" would promise what is not there yet. The foot says "open" rather than the concept's "run" for the same reason.
- **Results do not follow the replica while the finder is open.** On a busy board they would move under the selection; typing again asks again.
- **`⌘K` works from inside a text box.** It types nothing, and the concept does the same. Search 04 decides the shortcuts that do type.
- **The status bar's six tests of each replica state moved to `ui/frame/tests/status.tsx`, and the title bar's drag test to `ui/frame/tests/title.tsx`,** beside the code they test, so `ui/tests/application.tsx` stays under Biome's 300 lines with the finder's two tests of the whole path. The deploy thread both its pane tests use became one fixture.

**Verified by hand.** The release build, run under a home of its own, timed from the page with a temporary harness that was removed before the hand-over.

- **Online, on the 100,000-message board** (`vb serve` at 4200, 100,070 messages), from an empty replicas folder, load 2.9 while the queries ran. "Finder" is from the query typed to its results drawn on screen; "core" is the `search` command alone; each is the median and the slowest of 10:

  | Query | Finder | Core | Shown |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `work` | 17 ms (28) | 13 ms (19) | 50 messages |
  | `the` | 17 ms (19) | 12 ms (13) | 50 messages |
  | `w` | 17 ms (18) | 15 ms (15) | 50 messages |
  | `checking build` | 25 ms (29) | 20 ms (20) | 50 messages |
  | `"checking the bu` | 25 ms (26) | 23 ms (23) | 50 messages |
  | `47249` | 8 ms (9) | 1 ms (1) | 1 message |
  | `area1` | 8 ms (9) | 1 ms (1) | 11 channels |
  | `zzz` | 8 ms (9) | 1 ms (2) | No match |

  Every query is under the 50 ms budget. The 8 ms floor is the frame the results are drawn in, at 120 Hz.
- **On a copy of the same board** served on 4700, with two tasks created on it and one of them blocked (100,072 messages), load 4.7: the same times within 2 ms, `the` showing 2 tasks above its 50 messages, and `agent` 40 agents in 8 ms (9).
- **Typing faster than the answers**, every key at once: `checking build` (14 keys) asked the core twice, `c` (27 ms) and then `checking build` (18.5 ms), and its answer came 44.5 ms after the last key; `ship the www preview` (20 keys) also asked twice and was answered 13 ms after the last key. Load 5.6 to 5.8.
- **With `vb serve` stopped** (the copy on 4700, stopped by its PID): the finder kept answering from the replica, 13,869 searches in a row, for about 3.5 s while the core retried the board. Then the core declared the board gone, the shell dropped the replica, the app showed the start screen and the finder went with the board; a search after that answers "no replica is open". **The finder needs nothing from the board, but the app does not yet keep a stopped board open:** reading and searching it offline is Boards 02.
- **Looks:** the finder over Flow in the release app's window, and the finder with the real answers above (`build` with a blocked and a ready task, `area`, `agent`, nothing found, and the empty box) in dark and light, drawn from the built UI by a headless browser, compared with the concept's palette.
- **Measuring needs the window on screen.** WebKit holds a hidden page's timers, rendering and IPC, so a harness stalls while the window sits behind others. Bringing the window to the front takes the screen from the person using the Mac, and the window was closed from outside several times while it was there, so the typing and offline runs left it where it opened and turned WebKit's hidden-page suppression off for the measurement instead. The query times above were taken with the window on screen.

Left for later:

- **A found reply opens its thread at the top,** as a line selected in Flow does. In a long thread the reply can be out of view: the pane could bring the message it was opened for into view and mark it. That is the thread spec's, and it would change Flow and Tasks too.
