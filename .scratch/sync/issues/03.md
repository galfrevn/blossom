# 03: Resume after a restart

**What to build:** reopening the app on a board it has copied before resumes the feed from the replica's stored cursor, without copying again, so the board is ready at once. A cursor the board no longer accepts starts a new copy. A different board running at the same address, such as another project's `vb serve` on the same port, gets a replica of its own instead of being mixed into the old one.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A board's identity is its address plus the creation time and ID of its oldest message, and its replica is named after it.
- [ ] Opening a known board follows the feed from the stored cursor; a 400 `invalid_cursor` replaces the replica with a fresh copy.
- [ ] Tests come first: a restart resumes without copying; a refused cursor copies again; a different board at the same address gets a new replica.
- [ ] Verified by hand: restarting the app on the 100,000-message board does not copy it again.
