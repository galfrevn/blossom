# 06: The replica's logs of changes stay small

**What to build:** the writer deletes the rows of `task_changes` and `message_changes` that every open reader has read, as ADR 0024 decides. `arrivals` is kept whole. Today both logs grow for as long as the replica lives, and since Sync 05 every renewal of a claim adds to `task_changes`.

**Blocked by:** Flow 08, Sync 05

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A reader tells the writer how far it has read in each log, and the writer deletes up to the lowest point among the open readers, never inside a copy's batch and never while no reader is open.
- [ ] No reader misses a change: a change written while a reader catches up is read on its next catch-up.
- [ ] Tests come first, against a real SQLite replica: rows read are deleted, rows not read by every reader are kept, and a reader that opens after a trim builds the same Flow and Tasks as one that never trimmed.
- [ ] Measured on `vb serve` with an agent renewing a claim every few seconds for ten minutes: the two logs stay at a handful of rows, and what a trim costs is written in the comments.
