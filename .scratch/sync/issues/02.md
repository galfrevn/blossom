# 02: Keep the replica live

**What to build:** once the copy is done, the replica follows the board's feed. A message an agent posts reaches the replica in under a second, and so do edits, deletions, reactions, labels and task changes. When the board closes the stream, as `vb serve` does about every 280 seconds, the core reconnects by itself, resuming exactly where it left off. The status bar says "100,006 messages" while live and "Reconnecting" while it is not.

## Comments

Built on 2026-11-20, test-first. The core gained 13 tests (the feed 6, the SSE reader 4, the replica 2, the open board's follower 1), and the UI 1. The copy's 5 tests moved to `core/tests/sync.rs`, beside the feed's.

**Measured against a real `vb serve`** holding 100,006 messages, on an Apple M3 Pro:

- **A post reaches the replica in 96 ms to 1.9 s.** The spread is the board's. `vb serve` polls its own log, every 25 ms while events flow and up to every 2 s when quiet, and `curl` on the same feed saw the same delays. Blossom's own share is storing the batch: 0.02 ms at the median and 0.38 ms at the slowest, over 200 single-view batches into the 100k replica. So "under a second" holds for Blossom's part and not after a quiet spell on `vb serve`. `documentation/research/board.md` records the polling.
- **Across the board's stream close.** `vb serve` closed the stream at 280,070 ms, and the core opened the next one in the same millisecond. Posts after that arrived in 106 and 225 ms, and the status bar read "100,051 messages".
- **A board killed with `kill -9`.** It leaves its token file behind. The status bar said "Reconnecting" within seconds. Restarted, the board was found again, copied and followed, and a post arrived in 599 ms.

Decisions made along the way:

- **The count is kept, not scanned.** `count(*)` over 100,000 messages took 20 ms, on every batch. The replica now counts its messages once when it opens (21.6 ms) and adds the new ones as they are stored.
- **A stream that ends at once without moving the cursor counts as a failed connection.** The core then waits as it does after a refusal, so a board that keeps closing streams isn't asked again in a tight loop. A stream that lived longer, or brought events, is reopened at once.
- **A restarted `vb serve` mints a new token.** Found by hand: the app kept following with the old token and stayed on "Reconnecting". The open board's follower now reports the board again when the way to reach it changes, even at the same address for the same person, and a test covers it.
- **The cursor is stored with the views it came with,** in one transaction, and the copy stores its final cursor. A frame that brings no view and doesn't move the cursor, such as the bare `id:` that opens each stream, is not written.
- **The SSE reader is public** so its tests can cut a stream at every byte, including inside a multibyte character.
- **A fatal error leaves the last state on screen.** An event the core cannot read, or a replica it cannot write, stops the follower with a log line, and the status bar keeps saying what it said before. Ticket 04 is where the app learns to say a board is gone. The same applies to the copy since ticket 01.
