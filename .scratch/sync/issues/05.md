# 05: Renewals reach the replica

**What to build:** the copy and the live follow read Board's feed with `all=true`, so the bookkeeping events reach the replica: `task.renewed` keeps each claim's `until` current, and `actor.seen` keeps who's who. Today the feed hides them, so a claim keeps its first `until`, and on Vercel's hosted board, where a lease lasts at most five minutes, an agent that renews reads as lapsed after its first lease (found in Fleet 01).

**Blocked by:** Flow 07, which changes the copy

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The copy and the follow ask for `all=true`; a renewal stores the root's view it carries, and the task's `until` follows it.
- [ ] Bookkeeping events that concern no message (`thread.synced`, `message.synced`, `message.files_synced`) are read and skipped without error; `actor.seen` keeps the actors table.
- [ ] Tests come first, against the fake board: a renewal after a claim moves its `until`, and the fleet stops reading the agent as lapsed.
- [ ] Verified by hand on `vb serve`: an agent that renews its claim keeps its lease in the replica, and what `all` adds to the copy of the 100,000-message board is measured.
