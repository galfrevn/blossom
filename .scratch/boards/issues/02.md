# 02: Reading a board offline

**What to build:** when the open board is gone, the app keeps showing its replica, read-only and searchable, with "Offline · last seen 14:02" in the status bar and writing disabled. The core keeps asking the board at the pacing's slowest and goes live when it answers, from the stored cursor. With no board running, the start screen lists the known boards to read offline.
