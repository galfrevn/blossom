# 01: The board switcher

**What to build:** the title bar's board name opens a switcher listing the boards the app knows, the replicas on disk and the local boards running now, each live or offline with when it was last seen. Choosing one opens it and keeps the other's replica. The app reopens the board it had open when it quit.

**Blocked by:** Flow 01

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Each replica keeps its address, identity, person and when it was last live.
- [ ] The core answers the known boards through a subscription, and opens the one chosen.
- [ ] Tests come first: in the core against two fake boards; the switcher against the fake core.
- [ ] Verified by hand with two `vb serve` boards on two ports.
