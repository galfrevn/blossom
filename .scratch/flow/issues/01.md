# 01: The frame of the approved concept

**What to build:** the window gains the fourth concept's frame: the title bar with the open board's name and an inert finder box, the sidebar with the lenses and the channel tree, an empty lens area with its header, a detail pane that opens on a selection and closes with Esc, and the status bar as it is. The channel tree comes from the replica, with each channel's message count, and follows it as messages arrive.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The core answers the channel tree from the replica: every channel with messages, nested by `/`, with its count, through a subscription that follows the replica.
- [ ] The sidebar lists the lenses that are built (Flow only, for now) and the channel tree, with the concept's spacing, type and colours, in light and dark.
- [ ] The detail pane opens and closes; Esc closes it.
- [ ] The start screen for "No board running" stays as it is.
- [ ] Tests come first: the channel tree in the core against a real SQLite replica, and the frame in the UI against the fake core.
- [ ] Verified by hand against the 100,000-message board: the tree shows the 20 channels with their counts, and the budgets of ADR 0009 still hold.
