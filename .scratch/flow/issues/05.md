# 05: The sidebar's channels narrow Flow

**What to build:** choosing a channel in the sidebar narrows Flow to it and to every channel under it; choosing it again, or Flow itself, shows the whole board again. The lens's header names the channel.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [x] The core answers windows of the sequence narrowed to a channel and its subtree, and keeps the narrowed sequence in step.
- [x] The sidebar marks the chosen channel; the header names it.
- [x] Tests come first: the narrowed windows in the core, the choice in the UI.
- [x] Verified by hand on the 100,000-message board: narrowing and widening each take under 50 ms.

## Comments

Built on 2026-11-21. The core gained 3 tests and the UI 6.

**Verified by hand** against `vb serve` holding 100,065 messages, with a harness on a throwaway worktree: narrowing Flow to five channels took 8 to 19 ms, and widening it back 8 to 9 ms.

Decisions made along the way:

- **The reader numbers each message's channel** (a `u32` into the list of channel names) instead of keeping its name, and builds a channel's narrowed order when it is first asked for, again only when the flow has grown since.
- **Windows of the whole flow copy nothing:** a window maps positions through a function, so only a narrowed flow keeps a list of positions.
- **A folder of channels narrows too:** `fleet` covers `fleet/www` and `fleet/api`, but not `fleetwide`.
- **The list learns its total from the windows it reads,** narrowed or not, so `FlowChange` carries only a revision now.
- **Choosing the chosen channel again, Flow in the sidebar, or the × beside the header's "in fleet/www" widens it.** Each choice starts the list afresh at the newest message.
