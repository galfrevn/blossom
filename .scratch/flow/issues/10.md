# 10: Mentions read as names, and Flow's lines as plain text

**What to build:** a mention reads as the name of whom it mentions, `@claude`, in Flow's lines and in the thread pane, not as its stored form `@[local:user:galfrevn+claude]`. Flow's lines show a message's text as plain text: the markdown's marks go, its words stay, and a line keeps to one or two lines as it does today. The pane keeps rendering markdown (Thread 02).
