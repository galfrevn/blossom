# 03: Flow stays live

**What to build:** a message an agent posts appears at the bottom of Flow within a second. At the bottom, the list stays there; scrolled up, it keeps its place and says how many new messages wait below, and a click goes to them. An edited or deleted message changes in place. A message that arrives late with an older time goes in its place.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [x] The core keeps the sequence in step as the replica changes, and the subscription announces each change.
- [x] The list keeps to the bottom when it was there, keeps its place when it was not, and shows the count of new messages.
- [x] An edit or a deletion redraws its row; a deleted message reads as deleted.
- [x] Tests come first: the sequence after new, late, edited and deleted messages in the core; keeping to the bottom and the count of new messages in the UI.
- [x] Verified by hand: posts to `vb serve` appear within a second, and reading further up is not disturbed by them.

## Comments

Built on 2026-11-21. The UI gained 3 tests; the core's part (the sequence after new, late, edited and deleted messages) came with ticket 02's reader tests.

**Verified by hand** against `vb serve` holding 100,065 messages, with a harness on a throwaway worktree:

- **At the bottom:** three posts arrived in 1.2 s, the delay `vb serve` itself adds after a quiet spell, and the list stayed at the very bottom.
- **Scrolled up:** two posts brought the notice "2 new messages" 315 ms later, and the place being read did not move.
- **The notice** took the list to the bottom, where the fifth post was the newest, and went away.

Decisions made along the way:

- **The count is what arrived since the list left the bottom,** not since the last visit; unread state comes with Attention.
- **End goes to the newest once shortcuts exist** (Search 04). The list is not focusable on its own yet.
