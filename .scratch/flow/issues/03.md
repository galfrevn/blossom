# 03: Flow stays live

**What to build:** a message an agent posts appears at the bottom of Flow within a second. At the bottom, the list stays there; scrolled up, it keeps its place and says how many new messages wait below, and End or a click goes to them. An edited or deleted message changes in place. A message that arrives late with an older time goes in its place.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The core keeps the sequence in step as the replica changes, and the subscription announces each change.
- [ ] The list keeps to the bottom when it was there, keeps its place when it was not, and shows the count of new messages.
- [ ] An edit or a deletion redraws its row; a deleted message reads as deleted.
- [ ] Tests come first: the sequence after new, late, edited and deleted messages in the core; keeping to the bottom and the count of new messages in the UI.
- [ ] Verified by hand: posts to `vb serve` appear within a second, and reading further up is not disturbed by them.
