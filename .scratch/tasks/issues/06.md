# 06: The sidebar counts the open tasks

**What to build:** the sidebar shows, beside Tasks, how many tasks are open, as the approved concept does. It follows the sidebar's chosen channel and the replica.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [x] The count is the chips' count of open tasks, from the core, narrowed by the chosen channel, and it follows the replica.
- [x] It is drawn as the concept draws a lens's count, and it does not move the lens's shortcut hint out of place.
- [x] Tests come first, against the fake core.

## Comments

Built on 2026-11-23 by a subagent in its own worktree, test-first, on main at 3c320b1. The sidebar's suite gained 5 tests against the fake core. Four failed first: the count of the open tasks, the count narrowed to a channel and the channels under it, the count following the replica down to none, and the count sitting between the lens's name and its key. The fifth, that no count shows before the core has counted, passed from the start and guards that.

**What was built.**
- **Beside Tasks, the sidebar counts the open tasks.** The count is the chips' counts of the open states, from the same request the chips make (`read_task_counts`). It is narrowed to the sidebar's chosen channel and the channels under it, and read again at each `TaskChange`. The core and the shell needed nothing new.
- **Open means blocked, failed, claimed, ready and waiting,** the five states the spec and the lens put first.
- **It is drawn as the concept draws a lens's count:** Geist Mono at 11 px in the third ink, between the name and the key. The name gives way and the key keeps its place.
- **Read aloud, the button keeps its name,** "Tasks", and is described as "8,500 open tasks". The figure on screen is hidden from assistive technology, so it is not read twice.
- **The chips' counting moved to `ui/tasks/counts.ts`** for its second caller, with which states are open. The count beside a lens is `ui/frame/count.tsx`.

Decisions made along the way:
- **Failed counts as open here, though Board's own CLI does not count it.** `vb tasks` lists waiting, ready, claimed and blocked as open, so its count would leave out the failed tasks, which wait for a person. The concept counts every task that is neither completed nor canceled, and the spec puts failed among the open ones the lens lists first.
- **A state the lens does not know is not counted.** The lens lists it after completed and canceled. The concept would count it, but Board has no such state today.
- **The count shows once the core has counted, 0 included,** as the concept always shows Tasks' count. Nothing shows before the first answer, so it never flashes 0.
- **The count's drawing has a file of its own.** The sidebar was 279 lines of Biome's 300, so `ui/frame/count.tsx` draws the count and the sidebar only wires it, ending at 296 lines. The component decides by lens, so another lens's count goes there too; the concept gives Fleet one.
- **The count keeps its own state,** so an answer redraws the count, not the channel tree.

**Verified by hand** against `vb serve` 0.54.0 on port 4600. The release build ran under a home of its own. A temporary harness in the page, not in this change, chose each narrowing in turn and read the sidebar, the chips, and where each lens's key sits.
- **The board:** 10,000 tasks in 20 channels of 500, made with `vb tasks create --file`: ten under `fleet/`, and `security/scan`, `web/checkout`, `general` and others. Every tenth waits for the one before it. Through the HTTP API, six agent profiles completed 1,200, failed 160, blocked 160 and held 400 on hour-long leases, and the owner canceled 300. The chips read Blocked 160, Failed 160, Claimed 401, Ready 6,899, Waiting 880, Completed 1,200 and Canceled 300.
- **The count against the chips,** at load 4.8:

| Narrowed to | Beside Tasks | Open in the chips |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the whole board | 8,500 | 8,500 |
| `fleet` | 4,250 | 4,250 |
| `#area3` | 425 | 425 |
| `#general` | 425 | 425 |
| `security` | 425 | 425 |
| the whole board again | 8,500 | 8,500 |

- **The key keeps its place.** At every step, every lens's key sat at 181 to 197 px across the window. That held for Flow's, with no count beside it, and for Tasks', beside 8,500, 4,250 and 425. The count ended 9 px before the key, the row's gap, and the name gave way: Tasks' name ended at 130 px beside "8,500" and at 143 px beside "425".
- **What it costs:** `read_task_counts` took 1 ms at the median and 2 ms at worst through IPC for the whole board, and 1 ms for `fleet` and for `general`, 30 reads each. The page's clock reads whole milliseconds. The sidebar asks once per `TaskChange`, as the chips do while Tasks is on screen.
- **Following the board,** at load 3.6, with Flow narrowed to `#area3`:
  - five tasks completed in `#area3` made it 420, 2.0 s after the last write;
  - three canceled made it 417, 1.9 s after;
  - five completed in `#general` left it at 417;
  - two new tasks in `#area3` made it 419, 1.7 s after.

  Sync 05 measured 1.1 to 2.1 s for a change to reach the replica.
- **Against the concept,** in a picture of the window: "Tasks 419" with its key in its box, the figure in mono in the third ink, and Flow's key straight above it.
