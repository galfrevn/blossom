# 02: The Tasks lens

**What to build:** the sidebar gains Tasks. It lists every task, open ones first, in windows of fixed 30 px rows with the concept's columns: state, task, channel, owner, held by with a lease bar, and last change. Chips per state with their counts narrow it, a box filters by title, and the sidebar's channel narrows it too. Selecting a row opens the task's thread.

**Blocked by:** 01, Flow 01

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [x] The core answers windows of the ordered, narrowed task list, and the chips' counts, and follows the replica.
- [x] Leases count down in the UI and read as lapsed once past.
- [x] Tests come first: order, counts and filters in the core; the lens against the fake core.
- [x] Verified by hand on a board of 10,000 tasks made with `vb tasks create --file`: narrowing and scrolling stay under 50 ms.

## Comments

Built on 2026-11-21 by two subagents in turn, test-first: the first wrote the core and was interrupted when the machine shut down; the second finished it on main at 2338cc3 (Fleet 01, Search 01). The core gained 17 tests in `core/tests/tasks.rs`, and the UI 36: the lens 8, its table 10, a row 6, a lease 5, the clock 2 and the lens choice 5, with the sidebar's tests rewritten for two lenses and one application test added.

**What was built.**
- **The task index keeps each task's title,** the first line of its root, so an edit of the root renames it. Every insert and update of a task is logged in `task_changes`, by triggers, as `arrivals` logs messages. The schema is version 6, with Fleet's `tasks_by_claim`, so a replica from before is copied again.
- **The reader keeps the order in memory:** each task's state, last change, channel and lowercased title, sorted blocked, failed, claimed, ready, waiting, completed, canceled, then any state the lens does not know, and within a state the latest change first. It answers windows of the list narrowed by channel (with the channels under it), state and title, and the chips' counts for a channel, and reads each window's rows by ID. A narrowing is computed once and kept until the order or the narrowing changes. `catch_up_tasks` reads the log since its last change and puts the changed tasks back in their place.
- **The shell** follows the replica: each refresh catches the tasks up and announces a `TaskChange` when any moved. `read_tasks`, `read_task_counts` and `subscribe_to_tasks` live in `desktop/src/tasks.rs`.
- **The lens** has the concept's columns, a chip per state with tasks and its count, a box that filters by title, and the sidebar's channel. Rows are a fixed 30 px, read in windows of 100. Clicking a row or moving with the arrow keys selects a task and opens its thread in the pane.
- **Leases count down in the page:** one clock ticks each second while anything listens, and each lease and "updated" cell re-renders only when its text changes. Under a minute the bar turns amber, and past `until` the lease reads "lapsed" while the pill still says claimed.

**Decisions made along the way:**
- **How many tasks a waiting task still needs left the row.** The first subagent had put it in the core's row; ticket 04 asks for "needs 2 tasks", so it waits for that ticket.
- **Owner is the actor's handle, held by is its name** (`ada`, `claude`), and an ID the replica has no details of reads as the ID. A blocked or failed task's "held by" names who blocked or failed it, from its latest such event, as the concept draws it.
- **A lease's length is measured from its latest `task.claimed` or `task.renewed`.** When the replica never saw one, as on a board whose feed starts later, the bar measures against the 15 minutes Board gives when none is asked.
- **The chips show the states that have tasks,** in the lens's order, plus the chosen one at 0 when the channel has none of it. Choosing the chosen chip again widens.
- **While a narrowed list is read, the rows that answered last stay on screen,** so typing in the box never flashes an empty list. Rows are read 60 ahead of the screen and drawn 20 ahead: reading only what was drawn left a blank row in 2 of 358 frames at 30,000 px/s.
- **Columns drop as the lens narrows, Held by last:** Owner and Updated under 880 px, Channel under 680, Held by under 520. With the thread pane open in a 1,200 px window the lens is about 600 px, and the lease is what this lens is for. Updated is 72 px, against the concept's 64, so "1 h 03 min" fits.
- **Choosing another lens keeps the channel,** as the concept does, and choosing the lens on screen again widens it, as Flow 05 had choosing Flow do. The lens on screen stays the sidebar's current page while narrowed; Flow 05 had marked Flow as not current then.
- **Keys 2 and 3 choose Flow and Tasks,** as the concept shows them (ADR 0005), except while typing in a box or with a modifier.
- **The concept's state colours joined `ui/theme/tokens.css`,** `--state-ready` to `--state-canceled` in both themes, and Tailwind as `state-*`. The accent stays off them (ADR 0016).
- **The list is an ordered list of rows** with their position and the set's size, the lease is a native meter, and the chips a fieldset. Biome's rules ask for native elements over ARIA grid roles on divs, and an ARIA grid needs a focusable cell per row.
- **Shared with Flow:** the windows (`ui/core/windows.ts`), the arrow keys (`ui/core/arrows.ts`) and the pane that opens on a selection (`ui/thread/open.tsx`) moved out of Flow for their second caller.

**Verified by hand** against `vb serve` 0.54.0 on port 4600, with the release build run under a home of its own, timed by a temporary harness in the page that is not in this change.
- **The board:** 10,000 tasks made with `vb tasks create --file` in 20 plans of 500, one per channel (`fleet/area1` to `fleet/area10`, `security/scan`, `web/checkout` and others), 10 as the person, 5 as `planner` and 5 as `triage`; every tenth waits for the one before it. Six agent profiles then completed 2,500, failed 300, blocked 300, and claimed 1,000 on leases of 30 s to 1 h, and the owner canceled 200. The chips read Blocked 300, Failed 300, Claimed 930, Ready 5,046, Waiting 724, Completed 2,500, Canceled 200.
- **The copy:** the first rows 2.7 s after a fresh start, the copy included; 0.1 s with the replica kept.
- **The core,** through IPC: a window of 100 rows took 1 to 2 ms for every narrowing, at row 0 and at row 9,900, and the counts under 1 ms. The page's clock reads whole milliseconds.
